# Exercise: Designing a Simple Knowledge Graph

1. For each of the following situations, write down an ontology (a set of entities and relation between them) that represents the situation.

a) A Healthcare Ontology.
* Patients have diseases and symptoms
* Symptoms are caused by diseases
* Medicines treat diseases
* Patients are prescribed medicines

b) A manufacturing process.
* A product has components
* The components are provide by the suppliers
* Fitting a component requires specific tools

c)  Works of Art.
* Artworks are created by people
* Artworks depict events and/or people
* Events tale place at locations
* People take part in events

How is this example different to the others?
