1. Is the following argument correct? If not, find the logical flaw.

- If it is Friday, Siobhan always has fish for dinner.
- It is not Friday.
- Therefore, Siobhan will not be having fish for dinner.

2. Is the following argument correct? If not, find the logical flaw.
- If you have an MSc in AI then you will get a good job.
- Declan has a good job.
- Therefore, Declan has an MSc in AI.

3. Is the following argument correct? If not, find the logical flaw.
- If there is a fire then there will be smoke.
- There is no snoke.
- Therefore there is no fire.

4. Is the following argument correct? If not, find the logical flaw.
- If it is cold, John wears a coat.
- John is wearing a coat.
- Therefore, it is cold.

5. Which of the following statements are equivalent?
- Kerry carries an umbrella when it is raining.
- Kerry does not carry an umbrella when it is not raining.
- Kerry carries an umbrella unless it is not raining.


6. There are three houses in a row. Each of the houses has one pet (Cat, Dog, Hamster) and one car (Ford, Tesla, VW). From the following pieces of information, can you work out which car and pet go with each house?

1. The house with the Dog owns the Tesla.
2. The house in the middle does not have a Cat.
4. The hamster is not owned by the house on the left.
5. The house with the VW does not have a cat.
6. The house in the middle does not own the VW.


> **Solution**   
> 
> From (1) and (4) we know that the house with the VW does not have a dog or a cat, so it must have the hamster. Further, from (3) and (5) we know that this cannot be the left or middle house, and so we have:
> - Right - Hamster - VW
> From (2) we know that the house in the middle must have the dog, and therefore, from (1) we have
> - Middle - Dog - Tesla
> Finally, we must, from what is left over, have:
> - Left - Cat - Ford

7. [VERY HARD] This is a famous problem sometimes attributed to Einstein. It requires some quite involved logical reasoning, but can be solved with persistence.

1. There are five houses.
2. The Englishman lives in the red house.
3. The Spaniard owns the dog.
4. Coffee is drunk in the green house.
5. The Ukrainian drinks tea.
6. The green house is immediately to the right of the ivory house.
7. The Old Gold smoker owns snails.
8. Kools are smoked in the yellow house.
9. Milk is drunk in the middle house.
10. The Norwegian lives in the first house.
11. The man who smokes Chesterfields lives in the house next to the man with the fox.
12. Kools are smoked in the house next to the house where the horse is kept.
13. The Lucky Strike smoker drinks orange juice.
14. The Japanese smokes Parliaments.
15. The Norwegian lives next to the blue house.

Who drinks water? Who owns the Zebra?

>
> This is a very hard problem. You can only get to a certain point before you have to start chaining facts together to see how if they result in an inconsistency later. The full solution is shown below, we will work this out during the class:

> 
> | House | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | Nationality | Norway | Ukraine | England | Spain | Japan |
> | Drink |  **Water** | Tea | Milk | Orange Juice | Coffee |
> | Colour | Yellow | Blue | Red | Ivory | Green |
