Hi all,
 
As previously discussed in the Teaching teams meetings, as part of the coursework assignments in this academic year we will require students to set up and keep up-to-date a gitlab repo of their coursework. gitlab2.eeecs.qub.ac.uk.
 
Students should share the repo with the module owner.
 
We will mark the final deliverables of the coursework in the usual way; the main goal of the repo is to encourage students to engage with good development practice and take ownership of their work, and if necessary, to inform any investigation of academic offences (e.g. collusion, undeclared use of AI).
 
Students should be encouraged to commit to the repo regularly, as they progress through their coursework.
