Solutions to Exercise 3: Formal Dinners¶

  1. Express each of the following propositions in terms of the atomic propositions:

  • F: Sean ordered Falafel for dinner

  • P: Sean ordered Pizza for dinner

  • C: Sean ordered Cheesecake for dessert

  • I: Sean ordered Ice cream for dessert

    a) Sean ordered Pizza and Ice Cream
    b) Sean ordered Falafel and Ice Cream or Cheesecake
    c) Sean ordered Pizza but not Cheesecake
    d) If Sean orders Pizza then he also orders Cheesecake
    e) Sean always orders ice cream if he orders pizza, and never with any other main course.

Solution

a) P∧IP\land IP∧I
b) F∧(I∨C)F\land (I\lor C)F∧(I∨C)
c) P∧¬CP\land\lnot CP∧¬C
d) P  ⟹  CP \implies CP⟹C
e) I  ⟺  ∧PI \iff \land PI⟺∧P
